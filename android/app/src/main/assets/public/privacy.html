Data Sovereignty & Offline-First Guarantee

Arch Pad Pro operates on a strict on-device data sovereignty model. Your blueprints, sketches, CAD measurements, and project assets are processed and stored locally on your device without third-party tracking, advertising SDKs, or background telemetry.

01. Overview & Offline-First Architecture

Arch Pad Pro is built from the ground up as a standalone, offline-first architectural drafting workstation. When you create sketches, calibrate architectural scales, or annotate drawings, all vector strokes and project metadata are saved directly to your device's isolated IndexedDB sandboxed database.

The application does not require an active internet connection to deliver complete functionality. You can draft, edit, and export architectural plans anywhere—including remote construction sites, underground basements, and flights.

02. Information We Handle

  • Local Vector & Drawing Data: All canvas paths, dimension calculations, text annotations, and project workspaces reside strictly in your device's local memory and browser storage. We do not inspect, transmit, or monetize your creative designs.
  • Zero Advertising & Zero Analytics Telemetry: Arch Pad Pro does not incorporate Google Analytics, commercial tracker SDKs, profiling pixels, or advertising identifiers.
  • Optional Cloud Synchronization: If you intentionally choose to sign in with your Google Account, the app connects to Google Firebase Authentication and Firestore to synchronize drawings across your authorized personal devices. We never receive or store your Google account password.

03. Device Permissions & Purpose

  • Internet Access: Required strictly for verifying Digital Asset Links in Trusted Web Activity (TWA) mode and performing user-initiated Google Cloud Firestore synchronization.
  • Local Storage (IndexedDB): Required to persist project blueprints and canvas buffers between app sessions on your device.
  • File Export & System Share: Invoked only when you explicitly export high-definition presentation cards, PNG images, PDF prints, or .archpad backup files to your device storage or via the native Android share intent.

04. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, license, or disclose user data to data brokers. The following trusted platform services are utilized solely for essential runtime infrastructure:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Facilitates voluntary cloud synchronization and secure Google Authentication when activated by the user. Governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel Inc.: Delivers application shell delivery, static asset caching, and SSL/TLS HTTPS encryption.

05. User Control & Data Deletion

You maintain unconditional authority over your project data:

  • Local Erasure: You can delete any sketch or reset your entire workspace at any time via the Projects Dashboard or "Clear Canvas" feature. This immediately wipes the local IndexedDB records.
  • Cloud Erasure: When deleting a sketch inside the app, it is simultaneously removed from your personal Google Cloud Firestore database. For full account deletion requests, simply contact our support desk below.

06. Compliance & Store Certification

Arch Pad Pro is distributed in compliance with the developer program policies of the Indus Appstore / PhonePe Appstore and Google Play. It complies with COPPA guidelines and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
